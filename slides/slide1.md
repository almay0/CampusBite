#CampusBite
## University food ordering and management system 

### Project description 
CampusBite is a C++ app that gooing to make ordering food simple and easy for students on the university campus,students can see cafeteria menus, order their meals, make online payments and take their meals from the pickup locations. 

### Purpose
- Make ordering food easy for students
- Save a lot of time
- Offer an organized ordering and management system

### Problem
Students waste a lot of time waiting in lines just to order food and then another time looking at the menu and prices.
Cafeteria employees don't have an organized way to manage the orders

### Target users 
university students and cafteteria employees
